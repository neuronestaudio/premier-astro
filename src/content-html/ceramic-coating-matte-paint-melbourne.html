Matte Is Different

Never Use a Gloss Coating.

Matte paint gets its look from a textured clear coat — a standard gloss coating would ruin it.

A regular coating adds depth and shine, which is exactly what you don't want on matte. We use dedicated matte-safe coatings that protect the finish while preserving that flat, factory look.

Matte is also unforgiving — you can't polish out marks the way you can on gloss. That makes protection and correct washing even more important, and a coating makes both far easier.

  • ✓Matte-safe coating preserves the flat finish
  • ✓Protects against staining, fingerprints and water spots
  • ✓Makes washing safer — no polishing to fix mistakes
  • ✓UV and contamination protection without any gloss

FAQ

Common Questions.

Can you ceramic coat matte paint?

Yes — but only with a dedicated matte-safe coating. A standard gloss coating would add shine and ruin the matte look.

Will it make my matte car shiny?

No. A proper matte coating keeps the finish completely flat while adding protection and easy-clean properties.

Is matte harder to look after?

Yes — you can't polish out marks like you can on gloss, so protection and correct wash technique are essential. A coating helps a lot.
