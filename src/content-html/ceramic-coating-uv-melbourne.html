The Melbourne Sun

UV Is Brutal on Your Paint.

Unprotected clear coat under constant sun fades, chalks and eventually cracks.

Melbourne swings from scorching summer days to harsh UV even on cool days. Over time that radiation breaks down the resins in your clear coat — reds go pink, blacks go grey, and the finish loses its depth.

A ceramic coating adds a UV-resistant layer that takes the hit instead of your paint. It's the difference between a car that still looks deep and glossy in five years and one that's visibly faded.

  • ✓Keeps colour deep and gloss high for years
  • ✓Protects plastics and trim from going grey (with trim coatings)
  • ✓Especially valuable for cars kept outside or driven daily

FAQ

Common Questions.

Does ceramic coating stop paint fading?

It dramatically slows it. A coating blocks a large share of UV, so the clear coat underneath stays protected and the colour stays deep far longer.

Is UV protection worth it if I garage my car?

Even garaged cars get UV exposure driving in Melbourne sun. It's most valuable for daily-driven and outdoor-parked cars, but benefits every car.

What about faded paint — can you fix it?

Light oxidation can be corrected back to gloss before coating. Heavily cracked or failed clear coat may need respray — we'll assess and tell you honestly.
