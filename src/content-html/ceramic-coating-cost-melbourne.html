What Drives the Quote

It's Not Just the Coating.

Most of a professional coating job is the preparation — the coating itself is the last, quickest step.

A real ceramic coating job is priced on the work that happens before the coating goes on: decontamination, machine polishing and paint correction to remove swirls and defects. Skip that, and you've just sealed the flaws in for years.

That's why a $200 'ceramic' from a budget shop and a professionally corrected-and-coated finish aren't the same product — even if the bottle says ceramic.

  • ✓Vehicle size — a hatch is less work than a 4x4 or van
  • ✓Paint condition — heavier correction takes more hours
  • ✓Coating tier — 12-month spray vs a 5+ year multi-layer system
  • ✓Add-ons — wheels, glass and interior coatings

FAQ

Common Questions.

Why are some ceramic quotes so cheap?

Usually because they skip decontamination and paint correction, or use a short-life spray sealant marketed as "ceramic". A coating locks in whatever is underneath.

Is a more expensive coating always better?

Not necessarily — the right coating depends on your car and how long you're keeping it. A 3-year coating on a lease car can make more sense than a 5-year one.

Do you charge extra to come to me?

We're mobile across South-East Melbourne. Travel is factored into your quote up front so the quote you're given is the quote you pay.
