FAQ

Common Questions.

Can window film cut our energy costs?

Yes — solar-control film reduces heat gain through large facades, easing air-conditioning load and running costs, particularly on sun-facing glass.

Do you offer privacy and branding film?

Frosted, privacy and cut-graphic films are available for meeting rooms, partitions and shopfront branding.

What is safety/security film?

A tough film that helps hold glass together on impact, improving safety and slowing forced entry — popular for shopfronts and ground-floor glazing.

Will it reduce glare on screens?

Yes — it cuts glare on monitors and POS displays, improving comfort and productivity.

Can you work around business hours?

We can scope the job to minimise disruption. Get in touch and we'll plan the install around your operation.

Do commercial films come with a warranty?

Yes — quality commercial films carry manufacturer warranties; terms depend on the film and application.
