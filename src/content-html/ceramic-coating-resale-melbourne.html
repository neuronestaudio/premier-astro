Buyers Judge the Paint.

A deep, swirl-free, glossy finish signals a car that's been cared for — and justifies a higher quote.

When it's time to sell, a coated car stands out in the listing photos and in person. No swirl marks under the sun, no faded panels, no water spots — it simply looks like a newer car.

That perception translates to money. A well-presented car sells faster and for more, and a coating protects that presentation for the whole time you own it.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Does ceramic coating increase resale value?

It helps you achieve a higher sale quote by keeping the paint in top condition. Buyers pay more for a car that looks immaculate and well cared for.

Should I coat a car I plan to sell?

Even a pre-sale detail and enhancement can lift the sale quote. For cars you'll keep a few years first, a coating protects that value the whole time.

Is a coating transferable to the new owner?

The protection stays on the car. Handing over the coating certificate and any remaining warranty is a nice selling point.
