The Difference

Not the Same Thing.

Both get sold as "paint protection", but the product and the prep are worlds apart.

Dealer Add-On

Usually a low-grade sealant applied quickly over an unwashed, un-corrected car — swirls and all — as a finance-desk upsell.

Professional Coating

A named GYEON or ONYX coating applied over decontaminated, machine-corrected paint by a specialist, with a registered warranty.

The Result

A dealer sealant fades in months; a professional coating delivers years of gloss, protection and easy washing.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Is dealer paint protection worth it?

Rarely, in our honest opinion. It's usually a high-margin sealant applied without correction. The money is better spent on a professional coating.

I already paid for dealer protection — what now?

We can assess it, correct the paint and apply a proper coating over the top. Bring it in and we'll tell you exactly what it needs.

Why is the dealer version cheaper (or "free")?

Because the product and labour cost them very little. A professional coating includes hours of correction — that's what you're actually paying for.
