Comfort, Cost & Branding.

Large commercial glazing means big heat gain, glare on screens and fading stock — film solves all three.

Solar-control film keeps offices and retail spaces comfortable and reduces cooling costs across big glass facades. It cuts glare on monitors and POS screens, and protects displayed stock from UV fading.

There are also frosted and privacy films for meeting rooms and branding, plus safety and security films that hold glass together on impact.
