The Right Way

Wash It Properly.

Most coating problems come down to how the car is washed after it's coated.

  • ✓Use a pH-neutral car shampoo — never dish soap or harsh degreasers
  • ✓Two-bucket method with a soft wash mitt to avoid re-introducing swirls
  • ✓Rinse thoroughly and dry with a clean microfibre or blower
  • ✓Avoid automatic brush car washes — they scratch and strip coatings

Keep It Peaking

Boosters & Top-Ups.

A quick ceramic top-up restores water beading and adds gloss between washes.

A ceramic booster (like GYEON Q² DuraBead) is a spray-on product that refreshes the coating's hydrophobic properties and extends its life. It takes minutes and makes a coated car look freshly done.

FAQ

Common Questions.

How often should I wash a coated car?

Every 1–2 weeks is ideal. The good news is a coated car washes far faster because dirt doesn't bond to it.

Can I use a car wash?

Avoid automatic brush washes — they scratch paint and degrade coatings. Touchless or hand washing is fine.

Do I still need to wax it?

No — a coating replaces wax. If anything, use a ceramic booster spray, not a traditional wax.
