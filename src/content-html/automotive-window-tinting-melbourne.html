FAQ

Common Questions.

How dark can I legally tint in Victoria?

Victoria has VLT (visible light transmission) limits — front side windows must let through a minimum amount of light. We'll advise on a compliant, great-looking option for your car.

Does tint really reduce heat?

Yes — quality ceramic and carbon films are rated for heat rejection and make a real difference to cabin temperature and comfort.

How long does tint last?

Quality film lasts many years without bubbling or purpling, and carries a warranty. Cheap film fades and bubbles — which is why film choice matters.
