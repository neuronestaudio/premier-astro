Make It Last

Maintenance Matters.

The single biggest factor you control is how the car is washed.

Harsh automatic car washes, the wrong soaps and poor technique all shorten a coating's life. A simple two-bucket wash with a pH-neutral shampoo, plus an occasional ceramic booster, keeps it performing for years.

For lifetime ONYX cover, an annual maintenance service is required — we handle that for you.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Does ceramic coating last forever?

Most coatings have a rated life (1–5+ years). ONYX offers a lifetime warranty program, but that requires ongoing annual maintenance to stay valid.

What shortens a coating's life?

Automatic brush washes, harsh chemicals, poor wash technique and never topping it up. Correct maintenance is everything.

Can you top up an existing coating?

Yes — a ceramic booster refreshes hydrophobics and extends the life of your coating between full services.
