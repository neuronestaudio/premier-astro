WHAT IT IS

Ultimate Protection.
Years, Not Months.

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that bonds with your vehicle's factory paint, creating a layer of protection that lasts for years. Unlike traditional waxes or sealants that wash away over time, a true ceramic coating provides long term defense against UV rays, oxidation, and chemical stains.

Before any coating is applied, we perform a thorough decontamination and a single stage machine polish (paint enhancement) to ensure the surface is as perfect as possible. Locking in defects is not an option.

The result is a surface that is incredibly hydrophobic (water repellent), exceptionally glossy, and significantly easier to keep clean. Knowing your coating is applied by a certified professional means you can trust the durability and performance of the finish. It's the best investment you can make for your vehicle's exterior.

THE PROCESS

How It Works

01

Intensive Prep

A flawless coating requires a flawless surface. We chemically and mechanically decontaminate the paint to remove all embedded impurities.

02

Paint Enhancement

We machine polish the vehicle to remove light defects, swirls, and haze, maximizing the gloss before we lock it in.

03

Coating Application

We carefully apply the certified GYEON coating, working panel by panel to ensure even coverage, extreme gloss, and perfect bonding.

04

Curing & Inspection

The coating is allowed to cure, followed by a meticulous final inspection to guarantee a streak free, mirror like finish.
