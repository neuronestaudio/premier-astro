WHAT IT IS

Total Restoration.
Better Than New.

Paint correction goes beyond a simple polish. It is a multi-stage compounding and refining process designed to safely level the clear coat and permanently remove heavy defects like deep scratches, etching, and severe oxidation.

We start with an aggressive compounding stage to tackle the deepest damage, followed by a refining polish to restore absolute clarity, depth, and a mirror-like finish.

Because this process removes a microscopic layer of clear coat to fix the damage, we always follow it with a world-class ceramic coating to protect the newly restored surface for years.

THE PROCESS

How It Works

01

Assessment & Prep

We measure paint thickness and assess the severity of defects before performing a thorough decontamination wash.

02

Heavy Compounding

Using aggressive pads and compounds, we level the clear coat to safely remove deep scratches, etching, and heavy swirl marks.

03

Refining Polish

The compounding stage leaves micro-marring, so we follow up with a refining polish to restore absolute clarity and a mirror-like gloss.

04

Ceramic Protection

To preserve the flawless finish, we apply a professional ceramic coating, ensuring the paint remains protected for years.
