WHAT IT IS

A Real Upgrade.
Done in 4-6 Hours.

Paint enhancement goes far beyond a simple hand wax or detail spray—it's a dedicated single-stage machine polish. Before the polishing pad even touches your car, we perform a comprehensive chemical and mechanical decontamination to strip away iron fallout, tar, and bonded grime. This ensures the clear coat is perfectly bare and smooth before we begin.

Next, we carefully machine polish the exterior with a specialized cutting compound. This physically levels out the microscopic top layer of the clear coat where light scratches and swirl marks hide, resulting in noticeably sharper gloss and clarity.

To preserve this newly restored finish, we apply a premium 1-year ceramic coating. It bonds directly to the paint, offering robust defense against harsh UV rays, coastal salt air, and daily contamination.

THE PROCESS

How It Works

01

Wash & Decontamination

We start with a thorough hand wash, followed by a complete chemical and clay bar decontamination. (Access to a tap and power point is required). The surface must be flawlessly clean before any polishing begins.

02

Machine Polishing

We methodically work around the vehicle with a single-stage machine polish, panel by panel, to erase light swirls and significantly enhance gloss.

03

Ceramic Protection

A high-grade 1-year ceramic coating is applied to the paintwork and exterior glass. This creates a durable shield against the elements and makes future washing effortless.

04

Final Details

We dress the tyres, clean the glass inside and out, and conduct a meticulous final inspection. We don't pack up until the results meet our standards.
