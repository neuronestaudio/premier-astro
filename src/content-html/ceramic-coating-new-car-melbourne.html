New paint isn't as flawless as it looks — and it's already being attacked.

Even brand-new paint often carries fine transport marks and light swirls from the dealer's pre-delivery wash. We do a light correction first, then coat — so you lock in a corrected, perfect finish, not the dealer's.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Should I coat a brand-new car?

It's the ideal time. The paint is at its best, so a light correction plus a coating gives you the maximum lifespan and gloss for the least prep.

Isn't the dealer coating enough?

Dealer "paint protection" is often a low-grade sealant applied over unwashed swirls. A professional GYEON or ONYX coating on a corrected surface is a different level — see our ceramic vs dealer comparison.

How soon after delivery?

Any time in the first few weeks is great. We'll come to your home or work across South-East Melbourne.
