Clearer Wet-Weather Vision

Rain That Just Flies Off.

A hydrophobic glass coating (like GYEON Q² View) turns your windscreen into a water-shedding surface.

Above about 60 km/h, rain beads up and rolls off the glass without the wipers — a real safety benefit on the freeway in a Melbourne downpour. It also cuts glare from wet-road reflections and night driving.

Bugs, grime and hard-water spots are far easier to wipe away, and the coating helps resist the wiper-judder that comes with dirty, dry glass.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Does a glass coating really improve visibility?

In the wet, yes — water beads and clears off the windscreen at speed, so you rely less on wipers and get a clearer view.

Will it damage my wipers?

No. It actually helps reduce wiper judder caused by grime on untreated glass.

How long does glass coating last?

Typically up to a year or more with correct maintenance, depending on wiper use.
